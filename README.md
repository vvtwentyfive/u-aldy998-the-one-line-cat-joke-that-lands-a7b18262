## The joke

> My cat solved his new puzzle feeder in four minutes, then looked at me like I'd just handed him the tutorial.

109 characters, one line, nothing after the punchline.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the joke above
to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 109 bytes, a single line, no trailing
newline. The output is deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.

## Why this joke

The setup promises a clever human outsmarting a cat with a puzzle feeder.
The turn flips it: the cat cracks it in four minutes, and the look says the
whole plan was only the tutorial level. Fresh angle, real misdirection, and
the punchline is the very last word — a joke you can retell to a friend the
same day.
